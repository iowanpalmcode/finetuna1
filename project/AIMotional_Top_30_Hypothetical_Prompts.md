# AIMotional — Top 30 Hypothetical Prompts

These prompts are designed for AIMotional personality battles: simple enough to understand instantly, but open-ended enough for different personalities to produce noticeably different answers.

## Funny / Shareable

1. You accidentally send “I love you” to your teacher. What do you do?
2. Your friend says pineapple belongs on pizza. Respond.
3. You have $1,000 and 24 hours to spend it. What do you do?
4. Explain why you were late to school.
5. Convince me that pigeons are secretly intelligent.

## Advice / Life Decisions

6. I failed an important test. What should I do?
7. Should I quit something I've worked on for two years?
8. I have to choose between two colleges. How should I decide?
9. I don't know what I want to do with my life. What should I do?

## Debate

10. Is AI going to make programmers obsolete?
11. Is college worth it?
12. Should school start later?
13. Are grades actually important?
14. Is money more important than happiness?
15. Should humans colonize Mars?

## Social Situations

16. Your best friend is dating someone you don't trust. What should you do?
17. Someone hasn't responded to your text for three days. What do you do?
18. Your friend asks you for brutally honest feedback. How do you respond?
19. You discover your friend lied to you. What do you do?
20. Someone takes credit for your work. How do you handle it?

## High-Concept / Viral

21. You have one chance to become famous. What do you do?
22. Your best friend betrays you. What's your next move?
23. You wake up with $10 million in your bank account. What's the first thing you do?
24. You can delete one invention from human history. Which one?
25. You have 30 days to completely reinvent yourself. What's your plan?
26. You're the president for one day. What's the first law you pass?
27. You can know the exact date you will die, but nothing else. Would you find out?
28. You can instantly become world-class at one skill. Which skill do you choose?
29. You are given a time machine that can only travel once. Where and when do you go?
30. You can ask your future self one question, and they must answer honestly. What do you ask?
